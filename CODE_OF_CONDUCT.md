# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be technically direct, respectful, and evidence-oriented.

Healthy disagreement about methods, benchmarks, and conclusions is welcome. Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats, or sustained disruptive behavior are not.

## Research discussion

Critique claims, assumptions, measurements, and code—not people.

If a benchmark is weak, say why and propose a better test. If an experiment fails, document the failure rather than hiding it.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate this standard, and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

For private reports, use the contact path in `SECURITY.md`.
